# Product Specification: recurring billing

## Problem

Customers in the customer-support domain need recurring billing.

## Requirements

- Must complete within 10 seconds at p95.
- Must produce an auditable record for every state change.
- Must degrade gracefully when the downstream provider is unavailable.

## Out of Scope

Bulk operations and offline processing.

## Success Metrics

- Adoption above 36% within one quarter.
- Support contacts reduced by 14%.
