# Product Specification: instant refunds

## Problem

Customers in the payments domain need instant refunds.

## Requirements

- Must complete within 5 seconds at p95.
- Must produce an auditable record for every state change.
- Must degrade gracefully when the downstream provider is unavailable.

## Out of Scope

Bulk operations and offline processing.

## Success Metrics

- Adoption above 28% within one quarter.
- Support contacts reduced by 30%.
