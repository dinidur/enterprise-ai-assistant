# Product Specification: multi-currency settlement

## Problem

Customers in the data domain need multi-currency settlement.

## Requirements

- Must complete within 2 seconds at p95.
- Must produce an auditable record for every state change.
- Must degrade gracefully when the downstream provider is unavailable.

## Out of Scope

Bulk operations and offline processing.

## Success Metrics

- Adoption above 25% within one quarter.
- Support contacts reduced by 30%.
